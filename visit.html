What to Expect

Simple, reverent, joyful worship

Our worship is shaped by Scripture. We read God's Word, sing it, pray it, preach it, and see it in the ordinances.

You'll be greeted at the door, and someone can help you check in your keiki. Dress is island-casual. Stay afterward — we often share potlucks and fellowship in the breezeway.

The Lord's Supper is regularly observed and is open to baptized believers who are members in good standing of a gospel-preaching church.

  1. Call to WorshipGod summons His people by His Word.
  2. Praise & ConfessionWe sing His praise and confess our sin, hearing the assurance of pardon in Christ.
  3. Reading of ScriptureThe public reading of the Old and New Testaments.
  4. PrayerPastoral prayer for the church, our island and the nations.
  5. Preaching of the WordExpository preaching through books of the Bible.
  6. The Lord's SupperCommunion with Christ and one another by faith.
  7. BenedictionWe go out under the blessing of the triune God.
