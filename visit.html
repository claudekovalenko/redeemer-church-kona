What to Expect

Simple, reverent, joyful worship

Our worship is shaped by Scripture. We read God's Word, sing it, pray it, preach it, and see it in the ordinances.

You'll be greeted at the door, and someone can help you check in your keiki. Dress is island-casual. Stay afterward — we often share a meal and fellowship in the breezeway.

The Lord's Supper is observed regularly and is open to baptized believers who are members in good standing of a gospel-preaching church.

“O come, let us worship and bow down: let us kneel before the Lord our maker.”

Psalm 95:6
Order of Worship
  1. Call to WorshipGod summons His people by His Word.
  2. Hymn of PraiseWe answer with psalms, hymns and spiritual songs.
  3. Confession & AssuranceWe confess our sin and hear the pardon of the gospel.
  4. Reading of ScriptureThe public reading of God's Word.
  5. Pastoral PrayerFor the church, our island and the nations.
  6. Preaching of the WordExpository preaching through books of the Bible.
  7. The Lord's SupperCommunion with Christ and one another by faith.
  8. BenedictionWe go out under the blessing of the Triune God.
