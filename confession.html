Our Confession of Faith

The Second London Baptist Confession of Faith

We hold the Confession of 1689 — not as equal to or above Scripture, but as an accurate expression of the system of doctrine the Scriptures teach: an assistance in controversy, a confirmation in faith, and an instrument of edification and instruction.

About this text

The Confession was first published in 1677 by Particular Baptist churches in London and publicly owned by their General Assembly in 1689, after the Act of Toleration. It is closely related to the Westminster Confession (1646) and the Savoy Declaration (1658), sharing their Reformed doctrine of God and salvation while confessing a Baptist understanding of the church, the covenant of grace and believer's baptism.

The text and Scripture proofs follow the public-domain edition prepared by symbolics.dev, in both modernized and original spelling.
