What We Believe

The Scriptures alone are the final authority for faith and practice. They tell us what we are to believe about God, and what duty He requires of us.

I. Scripture Alone

The Word of God our only rule

The formal principle of the Protestant Reformation was that the Scriptures alone are the final authority for faith and practice. The entire Bible, from Genesis to Revelation, is the inspired, inerrant and authoritative Word of God. It is sufficient to thoroughly equip us for every good work (2 Timothy 3:16–17); we must not add to it or take away from it.

II. A Confessional Church

Saying plainly what the Bible teaches

We live in a day of doctrinal vagueness, so we want to be clear about what we believe the Bible teaches. We are therefore a confessional church: we hold the Second London Baptist Confession of Faith of 1689 as an accurate summary of the teaching of Scripture. We do not hold it as equal to or above Scripture, but as a faithful servant of it — linking us to the believers who have gone before us and guarding the pulpit for those who come after.

III. The Doctrines of Grace

Salvation is of the Lord

We rejoice in the sovereign grace of God in salvation: that all are dead in sin (Ephesians 2:1); that the Father chose a people in Christ before the foundation of the world (Ephesians 1:4); that the Son laid down His life for His sheep (John 10:11); that the Spirit effectually calls and regenerates (John 3:5–8); and that those whom God justifies He also keeps and glorifies (Romans 8:30). We are justified by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone — to the glory of God alone.

❦
IV. The Church & Ordinances

A Baptist understanding of the church

Christ is the only Head of His church. He gathers local congregations of baptized believers, governed by elders and served by deacons. Christ has given two ordinances: baptism, by immersion, of those who profess repentance toward God and faith in our Lord Jesus; and the Lord's Supper, a perpetual remembrance of His sacrifice in which believers feed upon Christ by faith.

In Brief

Our statement of faith

The Bible

The entire Bible, Genesis to Revelation, is the inspired, inerrant and authoritative Word of God.

2 Tim. 3:15–17; 2 Pet. 1:20–21

The Trinity

There is one living God who eternally exists in three persons: Father, Son and Holy Spirit.

Deut. 6:4; Matt. 28:19; 2 Cor. 13:14

Jesus Christ

Conceived by the Holy Spirit and born of the virgin Mary, He lived perfectly, died as our substitute, rose bodily, ascended to the right hand of God, and is coming again to judge the living and the dead.

Luke 1:31; 2 Cor. 5:21; 1 Pet. 3:18

The Holy Spirit

The Spirit indwells believers, convicts of sin, regenerates sinners to eternal life, and enables a life that glorifies God.

John 3:3–5; Titus 3:5–6

Salvation

All are sinners. Salvation is by the regenerating work of the Spirit, through repentance and faith alone in Jesus, by His shed blood.

Eph. 2:8–9; Rom. 5:1

Eternity

There will be a resurrection of the just and the unjust: eternal life with Christ for those who trust in Him, and eternal punishment for those who reject Him.

John 5:28–29; 2 Thess. 1:8–10
