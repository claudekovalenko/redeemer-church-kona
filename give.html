We give because He gave

“For even the Son of Man came not to be served but to serve, and to give his life as a ransom for many.” — Mark 10:45

The most selfless act in history was when Jesus gave Himself so we could have life. We are humbled by what He has done for us, and we tell people this great news and serve this community so more people will know Him.

We want to live generously so people can know the generosity of the gospel. Your gift furthers the spread of the gospel through the ministry of this church, here in Kona and around the world.
