The Baptist Catechism

A summary of the Christian faith in questions and answers, for the instruction of young and old. Commissioned by the Particular Baptists' General Assembly in 1693 and often called Keach's Catechism, it closely follows the Westminster Shorter Catechism and teaches the doctrine of the 1689 Confession.
